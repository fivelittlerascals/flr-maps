  16. 16Bicheno BlowholeTAS
    The blowhole’s spraying, Jack’s content, to watch it for an hourWe found just the place where we could stand to avoid a showerAddy had a rough sports day with three hits to the headSo when the time finally came, she was rightly quick to bed.
  17. 17Bicheno penguin rookeryTAS
    Half of us went to see the fairies come to shoreOut for the day, and in by dusk, penguins by the score.The smallest, flightless waterbirds the world has ever knownHave come to make their home in this sleepy seaside town.
  18. 18Wineglass Bay, FreycinetTAS
    The morning is for pool swimming, since all the kids do likeBut we cannot sit still for long so we head off for a hike.To Freycinet Park, it takes us half a dayAnd worth the uphill walk to see famous Wineglass BayThen the blowhole by morning, just can’t stay awayThe tide was low so not as mighty of a spray.
  19. 19Bay of FiresTAS
    Heading further north to see the Bay of FiresPark the car in the gravel lot because we don’t have sand tiresKids lounging in the lagoon, it’s slightly brown but not as coldParents jumping off the sand bank, because you can never be too old.
  20. 20Tamar Valley wineriesTAS
    Runs by the harbour then off to see vinesKids run in the sprinkler while the parents taste winesOne, two, three stops, we follow the signsFor the final seated tasting, the kids try to colour in the lines.
  21. 21Low Head LighthouseTAS
    Finally reach the lighthouse cottage as the sun is settingKids hopping on the rocks as they all are gettingTired of winetasting without any playgroundsNow surrounded by paddies, penguins and more ocean sounds.
  22. 22Platypus House, Beauty PointTAS
    Another animal stop, I’m sensing there’s a themeBut this time the focus is upon the monotremeA platypus couple in the midst of breeding seasonUnlikely for captive babies, so possibly without a reason.
  23. 23Gravelly BeachTAS
    We find a spot to have some lunch out on the Tamar riverFortunately warm enough so the kids don’t even shiverThey stage a vineyard protest—a detour not to takeInstead we simply spoil each one, with a chocolate shake.
  24. 24Seahorse World, Beauty PointTAS
    Back on the pier for seahorses early in the mornThousands and thousands of the creatures are weekly being bornThe males carry the babies, the ladies share the eggsFunny we call them horses when they don’t have any legs.
  25. 25LauncestonTAS
    We attempt a trendy lunch, out in LauncestonOh, the screaming and complaining, we simply should have known.At least the house we booked has a massive swimming poolBut it’s Tassie in the summer, so it’s mighty cool.
  26. 26Winery near LauncestonTAS
    To the kids dismay we attempt a winery by noonBut when they start to see the perks, they sing a different tune.Feeding apples to the pigs, and berries to the mummyThey’re more than a bit elated to put a fudge bar in the tummy.A pasta course, an evening swim, we finish out the dayEnjoy it while you have it, the last pool where we’ll stay.
  27. 27Stanley & The NutTAS
    Winery by 10, then straight off to StanleyClimbing the Nut without a chairlift, could make us seem manly.Resolved to eat dino pasta, too hard to eat outCold beach walk, jacuzzi tub—only if you don’t shout.Next morning dip our toes in northern watersHalf are sleeping, so Dad goes with the two awake daughters
  28. 28Devonportdeparture
    Pack it up, head for chocolate, a winery by threeChase through grapevines, piggy back, four filled with glee.
  29. 29Bass Strait crossingreturn
    Then it’s off to the ferry for playground and Paw PatrolThe kids sway watching TV, packed tightly like a shoal.We head ‘cross the strait, the waves banging on the hullThe cabin rocking back and forth, the rhythm doubling as a lull
