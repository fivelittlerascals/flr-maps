  3. 3UluruNorthern Territory
    The guides tell us the rock was an ancient riverbedSix kilometres wide, with a bit of iron, so it’s red.Flowing sediment down from the half billion-year-old Petermann ranges.When it oxides, it’s from grey to red colour that it changes.How it got vertical, is really what’s quite crazy.Although the rock just sitting there seems a little lazy.The sedimentary lines are erect at 89 degrees.The earth turned up this heavy rock, hardened from the pressures of the seas.Wild to imagine that this place was twice covered in waterFirst by a river, then an ocean, back when it was hotter.There likely weren’t Polar icecaps to hold up all the seasBut more recently the water dropped after a major freeze.Now the earth is colder, but Australia is dryerIn the middle of the day, the desert’s like a fryer.No activity, no animals, would venture out by day.They’re in burrows or nocturnal, is what our tour guides say.

  5. 5Kata TjutaNorthern Territory
    Kata Tjuta is the name for the collection of the dome.It is a mighty conglomerate where the Anangu men roam.Not for the women, but only men, this is a sacred place.Unclear what all they do, but at least the wild game they chase.
