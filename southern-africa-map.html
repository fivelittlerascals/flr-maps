2011 · 13 stops · Botswana, South Africa, Mozambique, Eswatini
  1. 1GaboroneBotswana
    The base for the trip, in the southeast corner of Botswana.
  2. 2eMalahleniMpumalanga
    The old Witbank, a coal city on the N4 and the last stop before the Lowveld.
  3. 3Mthethomusha Game ReserveMpumalanga
    A community-owned Big Five reserve on Kruger's southwestern boundary; game drives and San rock art in the koppies.
  4. 4Praia do BileneGaza
    A warm lagoon cut off from the Indian Ocean by a single line of dunes.
  5. 5Praia do TofoInhambane
    Whale sharks and manta rays year-round, snorkelled and dived straight off the beach.
  6. 6MacanetaMarracuene
    A cable ferry across the Incomati, then deep sand between the river and the surf.
  7. 7Malolotja Nature ReserveHhohho
    Highveld grassland and waterfalls, with a canopy tour on cable spans over the gorge.
    Malolotja Nature Reserve →
  8. 8NgwenyaHhohho
    Glass blown entirely from recycled bottles, in what was then Swaziland.
    Ngwenya →
  9. 9JohannesburgMelville
    Tin-roofed Victorian houses and pavement cafes in the old inner city.
  10. 10Hartbeespoort DamNorth West
    A dam in a gap in the Magaliesberg, and a morning with the elephants on its shore.
  11. 11Mokolodi Nature ReserveSouth-East District
    Rhino, giraffe and a cheetah visit, twenty minutes south of Gaborone.
  12. 12MaunNgamiland
    The flight up from Gaborone, and the gateway to the Okavango.
  13. 13XakanaxaMoremi Game Reserve
    A lagoon camp deep in the Delta, where mopane woodland meets permanent water.

    Private reserve near Hoedspruit known for hand-raised orphaned lion, cheetah and rhino that walk with guests.

    Orphaned hippo raised by a couple on the Blyde River who lived tame among them for over two decades.
  7. 7Camp Jabulani, Kapama Game ReserveHoedspruit, Limpopo
    Private lodge founded around a rescued herd of Zimbabwean elephant; elephant-back safaris at the time.
    Camp Jabulani, Kapama Game Reserve →
  8. 8Ngwenya Glass
    Recycled-glass studio near the Oshoek border — animal figurines hand-blown from old bottles.
    Ngwenya Glass →
  9. 9Mlilwane Wildlife SanctuaryEzulwini Valley

    Swazi Candles →
  11. 11Coral Divers, Sodwana BayiSimangaliso Wetland Park

    Coral Divers, Sodwana Bay →
  12. 12Durban
    Golden Mile beachfront and Indian Ocean surf city.
  13. 13Cape Town
  14. 14RondeboschCape Town

  16. 16Table Mountain Aerial CablewayCape Town
